Privacy

Effective 4 November 2026

KChess is a desktop app. It has no KChess account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and its developer receives none of your data.

What stays on your computer

Your games, studies, repertoire notes, puzzle progress, training drills and settings are stored only in KChess's folder on your computer. Lichess sign-ins are encrypted with a key kept in your system's credential store (Keychain on macOS). Deleting KChess's data folder removes all of it.

What KChess sends, and only when you use it

  • Lichess (lichess.org and its explorer, tablebase and puzzle-database servers): when you sign in, play, follow players, import games, look up an opening or an endgame, or download puzzles. What Lichess does with it is covered by the Lichess privacy policy.
  • Wikibooks: the name of an opening, to show its description.
  • OpenAI: only if you sign in with ChatGPT and ask for an explanation; the position or game you ask about is sent with your question. See the OpenAI privacy policy.
  • Downloads: the voice model from alphacephei.com when you turn voice input on, and, in the versions downloaded from GitHub, update checks and new Stockfish releases from GitHub. Versions from the Mac App Store are updated by the App Store.

These services see your IP address, as with any connection.

Microphone

Voice input is optional. Speech is recognized on your computer; audio is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Children

KChess collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
