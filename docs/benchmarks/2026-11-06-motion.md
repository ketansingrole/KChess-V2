Frames drawn per second are the same in every scenario: the bench steps every 400 ms, longer
than a slide, so every move still slides on both builds. `home-idle` draws once in 20 s, so its
CPU row is one sample's rounding. `local-clock` is pooled over two benches (3 and 5 runs per
build): it draws about once a second, and its p50 and p95 moved by ±15% between benches in
both directions, within the run-to-run spread on either build (draw p50 1.34–2.00 ms on main,
1.46–2.08 ms on the branch). `analysis-stepping` and `openings-shell` are from the 5-run bench,
the others from the 3-run one.

None of the changes adds work per frame: the board reads the executor's clock where it read
`Instant` before, the sliding piece's test registration compiles away outside tests, and the
reduced-motion probe runs at start and when a window becomes active. A keyboard toggle of the
sidebar now keeps its cached drawing instead of redrawing it for the easing period, and a held
arrow key no longer restarts a slide on every position, so both draw less than before.
